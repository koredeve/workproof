TITLE (paste into the Title field):
WorkProof — Freelance work. Verified by consensus.

DESCRIPTION (paste into Notes/Description, 993 chars):
A trustless work verification and settlement protocol. Clients create contracts with objective acceptance criteria and lock GEN escrow; freelancers submit evidence URLs; GenLayer validators retrieve the evidence from the live web and score every criterion PASS / FAIL / UNVERIFIABLE — agreeing on per-criterion results before the escrow settles automatically. Failed verifications protect the client's funds and open an appeal path: disputes are re-arbitrated strictly under the original, immutable criteria. Criteria cannot be changed after acceptance; double settlement is impossible; unreachable evidence is marked TRANSIENT and retried by consensus, never silently passed. Ships with a React frontend featuring real wallet connection (EIP-6963: MetaMask/Rabby/Phantom/Brave/Coinbase), a criteria-builder with explicit confirmation, an on-chain AI assistant that drafts criteria for client review, per-criterion verification result tables, a validator-pipeline visualization, dashboard stats, and a clearly-labeled demo mode. 22 contract tests + 33 frontend tests. Live on StudioNet: validators retrieved example.com and scored 2/2 criteria PASS with evidence-based reasoning, releasing escrow end-to-end.
